**Subject:** A one-parameter dark-energy law derived from your nested-black-hole framework — three questions on the clock correspondence

Dear Prof. Popławski,

I am an independent researcher. Building directly on your nested-black-hole / Einstein–Cartan framework, I have been testing a simple idea: if our universe is the interior of an accreting parent black hole, then the parent's accretion history $M_{\rm acc}(t)$ fixes an interior dark-energy equation of state,
w(z) = −(1/3)·(d ln M_acc/d ln t)/(H t),
which is the Croker–Weiner cosmological-coupling relation with an *external* source. A one-parameter power law $M_{\rm acc}\propto t^{\beta}$, $\beta\simeq2.4$, reproduces the DESI DR2 evolving-dark-energy signal and fits Planck+BAO+SN at the level of the best rivals with a single extra parameter. I have kept an adversarial log of the whole effort (including my own retractions) and pre-registered the DESI DR3 verdict under a public hash.

Nothing in the fit depends on the interpretation — but the interpretation is yours, and its two central debts are squarely in your domain. I would be grateful for your judgment on three points:

1. **The clock correspondence (my main theoretical debt).** The model needs the parent's accretion proper time to run *linearly* with interior cosmic time (an O(1) coefficient). I argue this via a time-reversed Oppenheimer–Snyder matching with the Israel junction conditions, finding $\mathrm{d}v/\mathrm{d}\tau \to 1/2$ at the horizon for marginally-bound ($E=1$) infall. **Does this linear clock-correspondence hold in Einstein–Cartan gravity with ongoing accretion?** Your 2024 extended-McVittie result — the horizon Hubble parameter differing between accreting and non-accreting cases — is exactly the kind of bridge I think this needs; does it support or constrain the argument?

2. **Homogeneous deposition (H3).** Does the torsion-bounce interior admit an externally-sourced dark-energy fluid deposited *homogeneously* ($\rho_{\rm de}=M_{\rm acc}/V$, with matter still diluting exactly as $a^{-3}$) without pathology? Is torsion-mediated particle production a viable uniform-deposition mechanism here?

3. **A clean discriminant (P4).** Your framework predicts rotation-axis and primordial B-mode (torsion-parity) signatures. My accretion mechanism is *isotropic*, so these signatures cleanly separate the rotation mechanism from the accretion one within the same framework. Is that a fair discriminant, and what is its current observational status?

And, simply: have I represented your framework correctly? I cite your 2010, 2016, 2024 and 2025 work throughout.

I would welcome any reaction — including that the clock argument is flawed, which would be the most useful answer of all.

With respect and thanks for the framework,
