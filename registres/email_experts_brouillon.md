Objet : Externally-sourced dark energy fluid from parent black-hole accretion — is this equivalent to a known model, and where does Birkhoff break the derivation?

Dear Professor Popławski,

I am an independent researcher (a former journalist retraining as a developer, so please weigh my claims accordingly). Working within your Einstein-Cartan black-hole cosmology, I explored a question you may find either trivially wrong or mildly interesting, and I would genuinely value knowing which.

If the parent black hole keeps accreting after the bounce, and if (big if) the infalling mass-energy manifests inside as a homogeneous component, then energy conservation with injection, d(ρV)/dt = Ṁ, fixes the interior equation of state completely:

    w(z) = −(1/3) · [dln M_acc/dln t] / (H·t)

i.e. the Croker-Weiner cosmological-coupling relation w = −k/3, applied one level up the hierarchy. For a power-law history M_acc ∝ t^β this is a one-parameter dark-energy model. Fitted to Planck 2018 (plik_lite) + DESI DR2 BAO + Pantheon+ (and cross-checked with DES-SN5YR and Union3), it gives β = 2.42 ± 0.07 (marginalised) and Δχ² = −12.6 relative to ΛCDM — matching w0waCDM's improvement with one parameter instead of two, resolving the DESI neutrino-mass tension the same way cosmologically coupled black holes do, and reproducing the DESI phantom crossing at z ≈ 0.36. Everything is reproducible (scripts and a 10-page note attached); the CPL branch of my pipeline recovers the published DESI DR2+CMB+Pantheon+ best fit to better than 1%, which is my main evidence the numbers mean something.

I am writing because two questions exceed my competence, and they decide whether this is anything at all:

1. IS THIS FLUID ALREADY PUBLISHED? It is not a standard interacting Q-model (matter dilution is untouched; the total interior stress-energy is not conserved). Its closest structural relative seems to be Josset-Perez-Sudarsky (PRL 2017): dark energy from energy non-conservation — but with a macroscopic, evolving source instead of their quasi-constant microscopic one. Your 2024 extended-McVittie result (H_hor differing between accreting and non-accreting black holes) is the nearest thing I found to a GR bridge for the mechanism. Does an equivalent externally-sourced w(z) model already exist in the Λ(t)/unimodular/diffusion literature?

2. WHERE EXACTLY DOES BIRKHOFF BREAK THE DERIVATION? Mass added outside a vacuum region does not gravitate inside it, so the homogeneous-injection assumption (my H3) cannot be naive attraction. Is there, in the Einstein-Cartan bounce interior, a known mechanism by which boundary mass flux sources a homogeneous interior energy density — or is this assumption fatal as stated?

A blunt "this fails because X" would be as valuable to me as any encouragement, and considerably more valuable than silence. Thank you for your time, and for the body of work that made this exploration possible.
