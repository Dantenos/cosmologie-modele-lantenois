\begin{abstract}
Systems that maintain themselves by processing their own substance --- autophagic cells, whole
metazoans, fire-adapted ecosystems, economic capital stocks, and (in a companion cosmological
model) a universe fed by its parent black hole --- appear to share a common, dimensionless
viability window. We define $x \equiv \tau_c/\tau_s$, the ratio of the independently defined
structural-renewal time to the self-processing time, under a pre-registered protocol with four
kill conditions (dispersion, controls, definition-sensitivity, edge universality) fixed
\emph{before} data collection. Across eight systems spanning $\sim$32 orders of magnitude in
mass, $x \in [0.37, 1.95]$ (0.7 decades). Inert or non-renewing controls fall at $x \le 10^{-2}$.
Documented deaths-by-rate bracket the window on both sides, including a natural experiment with
control (fire suppression vs.\ the unsuppressed Gila Wilderness). The \emph{sustained} upper edges
of four independent classes --- mammalian alimentary ceiling, cellular energetic bankruptcy,
generalized-second-law bound, ecosystem regeneration failure --- lie within 0.44 decades
(0.17 excluding the weakest), with all four mechanisms in the throughput/budget family. A
two-ingredient minimal model (clutter dynamics; linear maintenance cost anchored to the published
$\sim$20\% autophagic calorie budget) reproduces both bounds at order level. Finally, under the
guard-compliant definition for the cosmological case ($\tau_c = $ dilution time), the viability
variable is exactly $x = -w$: the dark-energy equation of state is the viability variable, with
$\Lambda$ the critical-maintenance point and the DESI phantom crossing the passage through
criticality. All kill conditions have been faced; none has fired. We state plainly what would
still kill the hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and protocol}
Any system that persists by digesting its own substance seems to face two deaths: too slow, and
un-processed damage accumulates (clutter); too fast, and the system devours itself (autosis-like
failure). We ask whether, once non-dimensionalized, the viable band is \emph{common across scales}
--- a law --- or merely the dimensional truism that homeostasis is $O(1)$.

\paragraph{The variable.} $x \equiv \tau_c/\tau_s$, where $\tau_s$ is the time to process one
self-equivalent of substance (or energy) at the current rate, and $\tau_c$ is the required
structural-renewal time, defined \emph{independently} of the flux (spontaneous component decay,
obsolescence, dilution). Systems whose $\tau_c$ can only be defined circularly (e.g.\ epithelia,
where renewal time \emph{is} the flux time) are excluded by a tautology guard. Two admissible
readings are declared and labelled per system: $x_{\rm self}$ (strict self-processing: cell,
universe, fire, capital) and $x_{\rm thru}$ (total throughput normalized to self-content: whole
mammal, whose basal $\tau_s$ is alimentary). The two readings join physically at the alimentary
ceiling, where saturated external intake forces any excess expenditure to become
self-consumption \cite{Thurber2019}; where both are computable they agree within a factor $\sim$2.

\paragraph{Pre-registered kill conditions.} (M1) post-guard dispersion of living systems
$>2$ decades; (M2) controls (non-renewing consumers, inert objects) inside the window as often
as outside; (M3) any defensible alternative $\tau_c$ moving a system across the window by
$>1$ decade; (M4, added before the edge data were collected) sustained death-edges of different
classes scattered by $>1$ decade.

\section{Deaths by rate, and the edge rail (M4)}
Documented rate-deaths bracket the window on both sides. \emph{Low side}: a century of fire
suppression in western US dry forests stretched the effective interval $3$--$6\times$
($x \sim 0.15$--$0.3$), yielding $2.9$--$13.6\times$ more stand-replacing fire and conversions
to non-forest, while the unsuppressed Gila Wilderness ($x$ maintained $\sim$0.8) sits at only
$1.8\times$ --- a natural experiment with control \cite{Parks2023}; ageing neurons, unable to
dilute damage mitotically, drift below the window into aggregate accumulation and a
senescence--autophagy vicious circle, gene-dose dependent and rapamycin-reversible
\cite{Neuron2024}. \emph{High side}: short-interval boreal reburns ($x \sim 3$--$4$) cause
regeneration failure \cite{Boreal2025}; sustained starvation-level autophagy kills a
dose-dependent subpopulation (autosis) at the window's edge \cite{Liu2013}; and hierarchy
offspring with $\beta > 4.35$ ($x > 1.53$) are GSL-unviable \cite{PaperB}.

\paragraph{The sustained-edge rail.} Four classes on one rail --- honestly labelled: two edges are external and measured (mammalian ceiling, boreal regeneration; these two alone span 0.43 decades), two are derived within our own frameworks (GSL, cellular budget); the content is the internal--external concordance
(Table~2): mammalian indefinite ceiling $2.5\times$ basal \cite{Thurber2019,HD1997,DrentDaan}
$\Rightarrow x \simeq 1.3$; GSL bound $x \le 1.53$; cellular energetic bankruptcy $x \simeq 1.9$;
boreal regeneration $x \simeq 3.5$ (weakest). Scatter: \textbf{0.44 decades} (0.17 without the
boreal case) --- M4 passes. A two-tier structure emerges: the ceiling \emph{decreases with
exposure duration} (7$\times$ basal for days, 2.5$\times$ indefinitely, measured in humans
\cite{Thurber2019}; transiently survivable starvation vs.\ sustained autosis in cells; the GSL
bounding the attractor, i.e.\ the sustained regime, by construction). Transient excursions live
at $x \sim 2$--$4$; the true, sustained edge at $x \sim 1.3$--$2$. All four mechanisms belong to
the throughput/budget family: the concordance is mechanistic, not merely numerical.

\section{A minimal model derives the window}
\emph{Lower bound (clutter).} Components damage spontaneously at rate $1/\tau_c$;
self-processing at rate $x/\tau_c$ removes material with selectivity $\sigma$ for damaged units.
The steady state $(1-d)(\sigma d + 1 - d) = x\sigma d$ drives the damaged fraction $d^*$ toward
saturation below a threshold: for $\sigma \in [3,10]$, tolerable damage $d_{\max} \in [0.5,0.7]$,
$x_{\min} = 0.31$--$0.66$ (illustrative; the biological vicious circle corresponds to a
machinery feedback deliberately left outside this minimal model). \emph{Upper bound (budget),
anchored to a sourced number.} With linear maintenance cost and the published $\sim$20\% of
caloric intake devoted to autophagic renewal at $x_{\rm basal} = 0.37$ \cite{PMC636}, the entire
budget is devoured at $x_{\max} = 0.37/0.20 = 1.85$ --- and the observed autotic edge
($\sim$1.95) sits 5\% \emph{beyond} the bankruptcy point: dying just past 100\% of budget is
the expected physics (transient excursions possible, sustained regime impossible). Order-level
agreement from an independent number (nominal 5\%; realistic uncertainties $\pm 30$\% on both
sides; the 20\% figure is organismal, applied here at cell level --- order-consistent).

\section{The identity $x = -w$}
For the cosmological member, the guard-compliant degradation is \emph{dilution}: unmaintained,
the injected density would redshift as $a^{-3}$; feeding maintains it. With
$\tau_c = 1/(3H)$ and $\tau_s = t/\beta$, the viability variable is
\begin{equation}
x \;=\; \frac{\beta}{3Ht} \;=\; -\,w \qquad \text{(exactly)},
\end{equation}
and the identity is general under the declared convention of a pressureless bare substance:
for any dark fluid, $-w$ is the fraction of dilution compensated by maintenance
($\rho \propto a^{-3(1+w)}$). Dictionary: $\Lambda$ is \emph{exactly critical maintenance}
($x=1$); phantom is over-maintenance (the autotic side); quintessence is under-maintenance
(clutter side); the DESI phantom crossing is \emph{the passage through criticality} --- the
universe over-maintained, has under-maintained since $z \approx 0.4$--$0.5$, and today sits at
$x = 0.85$, in the heart of the living pack. Formal kin: a recent thermodynamic reconstruction
writes $w = -1 + \dot S_h/(3H S_h)$ \cite{Reco2026} (rate-ratio identity on the horizon side,
where ours is on the substance side); classical fluid-entropy positivity requires $w \ge -1$
\cite{Duarte2019}, whereas our total-entropy GSL bound ($w \ge -1.53$) is more permissive ---
a contentful difference; and the $-1$ crossing is thermodynamically a smooth crossover, not a
phase transition \cite{Crossing}, consistent with the criticality reading. The window bounds
become bounds on $w$: \textbf{viability $\Leftrightarrow$ $w \in [-1.53, -0.35]$} (GSL +
depth-freezing), with observed dark energy at $-0.85$, mid-window (the $\beta\to w$ map uses $Ht$ fixed at best fit, a $\sim$10--15\% effect over the window). The universe's full life trajectory can then be read in the window: $x(z) = -w(z)$ starts at $1.35$ in the deep past --- $\sim 88\%$ of its own sustained (GSL) edge --- below it within current uncertainties ($\pm$10--15\% on the map, $\pm 0.07$ on $\beta$) --- descends through criticality at $z = 0.46$, sits at $0.85$ today, and settles on the attractor at $0.55$: an entire cosmic history lived \emph{inside} the sustained window, edge-proximal in youth, pack-central in age. Both faces are declared:
the identity is partly definitional ($w \sim -1 \Rightarrow x \sim 1$), and its content lies in
the derived bounds and the shared vocabulary.

\section{Ancestry, limits, and what would kill this}
The window's ancestors are the rate-of-living hypothesis \cite{Pearl1928,Speakman2005} --- of
which $x$ is the corrected version, normalized to required maintenance time rather than lifespan
--- and the disposable soma \cite{Kirkwood1977}, of which our upper bound is the quantitative
form: $x_{\max}$ is where the maintenance/function trade-off becomes impossible
(cf.\ the prudent-parent sustained scope \cite{DrentDaan}). Remaining honestly open: four of
eight rows are order-of-magnitude (basal cell flux mixed-status); the quantitative autosis
threshold is unpublished; the boreal edge is the rail's weakest; the survivor-bias concern is
addressed by the deaths and the Gila control but not eliminated; and the identity's definitional
face invites the tautology back through the cosmological door. The hypothesis dies if: a
guard-compliant system class is found far outside $[0.3, 2]$; the sustained edges of new classes
scatter the rail beyond a decade; or the two-tier duration structure fails in a class where it
is measurable. Each is a concrete assignment. A final stress test deserves record: hibernation --- months at 1--5\% of basal metabolism, far below the window --- proves to be the window's \emph{periodic solution} rather than its counterexample: damage accrues during torpor while cold-inactivated processing machinery idles, survivable torpor duration is bounded (\emph{the} classic mystery), and the costly interbout arousals restore accumulated metabolites at every cycle, with a repayment/debt ratio spanning $[0.7, 4]$ over the full plausible parameter grid ($Q_{10}$ damage slowdown, arousal processing rate): \emph{compatible} with balance, not a demonstrated closure. Attribution is owed: homeostasis restoration is the leading \emph{published} explanation of arousals (Carey et al.\ 2003; metabolomic confirmation 2011), and the bout-length--temperature relation belongs to that literature; the window's contribution is only the dimensionless bookkeeping placing this mechanism on the same axis as autosis and the GSL bound. Systems that cannot reside in the window transit it periodically.
